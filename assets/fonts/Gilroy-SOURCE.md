Gilroy by Radomir Tinkov

Official free distribution: https://www.tinkov.info/gilroy.html
Download: https://www.tinkov.info/downloads/Gilroy-FREE.zip

The project owner supplied the full TTF family from Downloads/gilroy.
The Gilroy family registers Thin (100) through Black (900), with matching
italic variants. Heavy and HeavyItalic are registered as GilroyHeavy (900)
to keep them distinct from Black within Flutter's supported weight range.
The supplied license is preserved as Gilroy-Supplied-License.txt.

The supplied TTF exports all declared OS/2 weight 400 internally. Their weight,
bold and italic metadata has been normalized to match the registered faces;
glyph outlines and spacing are unchanged. Reproduce with
`node scripts/normalize_gilroy.cjs`. The script also updates font checksums.

The original free Light and ExtraBold OTF files are retained for compatibility.

The original accompanying license is preserved as Gilroy-License.pdf.
