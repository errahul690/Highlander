Gilroy by Radomir Tinkov

Official free distribution: https://www.tinkov.info/gilroy.html
Download: https://www.tinkov.info/downloads/Gilroy-FREE.zip

This app bundles the original Gilroy Light (300) and ExtraBold (800) files.
Flutter uses these available weights for the Gilroy text family throughout
the native app. Other weights are not included in the official free download.

The original accompanying license is preserved as Gilroy-License.pdf.
